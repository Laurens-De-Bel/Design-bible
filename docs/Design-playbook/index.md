# Design playbook

The design playbook collects Ad Rem's mechanical design rules and conventions in one place, so machines and structures get built the same way regardless of who's designing them.

Use it as a reference while modelling: naming and structure conventions, fastener and hole standards, sheet metal and welding rules, and the small database/classification agreements that keep everything consistent across projects.
